# Cloth NeXt 2.8.3

Cloth NeXt 2.8.3 refines Pull to Resume into a single compound Bake control. When a verified recovery checkpoint exists, a green segment with a clock and its exact frame slides out from behind Bake while the containing toolbar background expands with it.

The Resume segment is completely absent when recovery is unavailable. Its gesture continues to delegate to the existing verified recovery operator and retains the guarded Pull to Resume lifecycle introduced in 2.8.1.

Published to the configured private repository. The external PPF Contact Solver is not bundled.
