01 / BETA 2.2.0

The timeline finally tells the truth

More motion samples.
Not more fake frames.

Earlier builds could interpret dense animated-collider samples as full logical solver frames. That stretched the physical timeline, slowed bakes and made collision behavior harder to trust. Beta 2.2.0 fixes the timebase at the pipeline level.
