01 / THE DIFFERENCE

Built around the solve

Technical power underneath.
Blender logic on top.

PPF is powerful. Preparing command-line jobs, moving scene data between applications and rebuilding the same setup every time is not.

Cloth NeXt turns the handoff into a visual, guarded workflow: assign roles, choose materials, validate, bake and review the result without leaving the context of your scene.
