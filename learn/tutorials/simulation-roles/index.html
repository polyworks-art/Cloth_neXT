Choose the Right Simulation Role

ClothTriangle shell deformation and garment simulation.
Rod / CableOne-dimensional curve simulation for cables and ropes.
Soft BodyVolumetric deformation from a closed manifold mesh.
Rigid BodySolid moving body behavior for supported scenes.
ColliderStatic or animated collision geometry.
ForceScene gravity, wind and aerodynamic controls.
