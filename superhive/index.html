CURRENT BETA SCOPE

Know exactly what you are buying.

Cloth NeXt is currently focused on offline production simulation for supported Windows and NVIDIA systems.

Included workflows

Cloth, Cable / Rope, Soft Body, Rigid Body, Collider, Force, Hard and Soft Pins, Collision Cages, Bake Companion and verified recovery.

Current platform

Blender 5.0 or newer, Windows x64 and a compatible NVIDIA GPU. The solver is designed for offline simulation, not real-time playback.

Cloth NeXt and PPF are separate projects.

Cloth NeXt provides the Blender workflow. The PPF Contact Solver is maintained and distributed separately by ST Tech / ZOZO and is never bundled with the add-on.
